# Sourcing Guide

Prefer official and legal sources first:

1. Constitution and statutes for institutions, offices, and legal mandates.
2. INEC pages, manuals, registers, and portals for election data.
3. State electoral commission or state government sources for local elections.
4. Government portals for administrative geography.
5. Academic, NGO, media, or community sources only when official sources are unavailable.

Every record must include `sources`. Use `confidence` to show how directly the source supports the record:

- `HIGH`: direct official or legal support.
- `MEDIUM`: source-backed but indirect, or migrated seed data awaiting official verification.
- `LOW`: weak source support.
- `UNVERIFIED`: known placeholder or unsourced contribution.

Records without direct official support should use `quality.status: NEEDS_REVIEW` and an issue explaining what must be verified.
